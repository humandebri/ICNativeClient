# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone. Be respectful, assume good faith, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and avoid personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety or constructive operation of the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
